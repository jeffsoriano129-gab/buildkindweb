Why I started BuildKind Web

A business website can help people understand what you offer, build trust, and make it easier for customers to get in touch.

But the process of getting one made can sometimes feel complicated. Business owners may be unsure which services they need, what they will be charged for, or who will own and manage the website once it is finished.

BuildKind Web was created to make that process simpler.

With more than 15 years of experience as a virtual assistant and web developer—including building WordPress and HTML websites for agencies and their clients—I bring practical experience to building websites for different business needs. My focus is on clear communication, thoughtful design, and responsive websites that work across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

My goal is to give small businesses a more approachable way to get online—without the unnecessary complexity or overhead that can come with a traditional agency process. You can discuss your needs directly with the person building your website, understand what is included before work begins, and choose the services that make sense for your business.

I also believe you should have clarity and control over your own website. Where possible, your domain, hosting, and third-party subscriptions should remain in your name, with costs explained upfront. You should know what you are paying for and how your website is managed.

Whether you need a focused landing page or a multi-page business website, BuildKind Web aims to make the experience clear, collaborative, and hassle-free—with professional design, responsive layouts, and practical solutions built around your goals.
