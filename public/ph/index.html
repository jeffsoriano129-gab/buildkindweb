WordPress

Built with Elementor, Divi or another suitable setup. Best if you want to update pages, posts and content yourself without touching code.

Questions

Good to know before you start.

What do I pay for separately?

Unless the proposal says otherwise, you purchase and renew your domain, hosting, premium themes, builders and plugins, business email, paid form, booking or payment services, and stock photos or fonts. You keep ownership of and access to these accounts.

Do I need to provide the content?

Yes. You supply accurate business information, text, logos, images and any legal policies. Copywriting, logo design and image sourcing are not part of core packages and can be quoted separately.

WordPress or static HTML: which should I choose?

Choose WordPress if you want to edit content yourself. Choose static HTML for a fast, lightweight site whose content rarely changes. We will recommend one during the discovery call.

What if I need more than the package includes?

Extra pages, sections, features or revision rounds are quoted first and only start once you approve the cost.

Can you guarantee Google rankings?

No. Basic SEO setup gives your site a sound technical foundation, but no ranking, traffic or sales result is guaranteed.
