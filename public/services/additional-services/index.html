Basic on-page SEO

Included in eligible packages

Help organizing page titles, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text, internal links, and other basic page-level SEO elements.

Pricing: Included at the level described in eligible website packages; larger existing sites or expanded SEO work are quoted separately. This is not an ongoing SEO campaign and does not guarantee rankings.

Contact forms and lead capture

Included or quoted

Set up a suitable contact or enquiry form and connect it to an agreed destination, such as email or a supported form service. More advanced workflows may require a paid third-party provider.

Pricing: Basic form setup may be included when specified in the website package. CRM connections, conditional logic, automations, and paid service subscriptions are quoted separately.

Website content and page additions

$50 USD per page

Add standard pages or make agreed content updates to an existing website.

Pricing: Additional standard pages for new website packages are $50 per page. Existing-site updates are quoted according to the amount of content, formatting, and technical work required.
