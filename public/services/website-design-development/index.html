Platforms and build approach

The right platform for your needs

The recommended approach depends on your content, functionality, editing needs, and budget.

WordPress

Built with a suitable page builder, such as Elementor or Divi. A good fit if you want to edit content through a dashboard or use a plugin-based workflow.

Static HTML, CSS & JavaScript

A lightweight build deployed through a platform such as Cloudflare Pages. Suits simpler websites where speed, simplicity, and low infrastructure costs are priorities.

Good to know

Clear expectations from the start

  • Prices are starting package prices in USD and apply to the scope described in the written quote.
  • Domain registration, hosting, email, premium themes and plugins, licences, APIs, and other third-party services are paid separately by the client.
  • E-commerce, membership systems, booking engines, custom applications, complex integrations, extensive data entry, and copywriting are not included unless specifically listed in the quote.
  • The client supplies final text, branding, and images unless content creation is included in writing.
  • Basic on-page SEO setup does not guarantee search rankings, traffic, or enquiries.

Transparency

Transparent pricing and ownership

BuildKind Web aims to make the costs and responsibilities clear before a project begins.

One-time build prices: Website packages are priced according to the stated scope.
Third-party costs stay separate: Domain, hosting, email, premium plugins, licences, and external services are paid by the client.
Client account ownership: Where practical, the client owns their domain, hosting, and third-party accounts and controls billing.
Written scope: The proposal confirms what’s included, what’s excluded, the timeline, and the payment schedule.
No ranking promises: SEO setup is a foundation, not a guarantee of search results or business outcomes.

The standard payment structure is a 50% deposit to schedule the project and 50% before launch or final handover, unless the written quote states otherwise.
